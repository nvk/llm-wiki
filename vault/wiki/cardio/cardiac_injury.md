Traumatic aortic injuries, when evaluated with mul­

grading of aortic injury has implications for clinical man­
agement. MDCT grades 0 and 1 reliably exclude aortic 
injury and do not require aortography. MDCT grades 3 
and 4 reliably confirm aortic injury. Only MDCT grade 
2 injuries require aortography to confirm or exclude the 
diagnosis of aortic injury (Fig. 6-24). Grade 3 injuries 
may, at the discretion of the surgeon, progress to aortog­
