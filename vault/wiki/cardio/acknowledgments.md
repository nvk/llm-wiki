## Textbook Reference Text
Acknowledgments
Many talented and creative people contributed to the third edi-
tion of Cardiac Imaging: THE REQUISITES. The ﬁ eld of cardiac 
imaging has grown considerably since 2005, when the last edition 
was published, so the scholarly efforts of the new authors are 
greatly appreciated.
THE REQUISITES series was conceived by Dr. James 
H. Thrall, Radiologist-in-Chief at the Massachusetts General 
Hospital and the Juan M. Taveras Professor of Radiology at 
Harvard Medical School. Drs. Stephen Wilmot Miller and Suhny 
Abbara thank him for continuing to support their academic 
activities in the area of cardiovascular imaging.
Two colleagues with many years of collaboration have exten-
sively revised their chapters. Dr. Mary Etta E. King, who 
founded the pediatric echocardiography section at the 
Massachusetts General Hospital, and Dr. Kaitlyn My-Tu Lam 
have refreshingly updated the voluminous new literature on 
ultrasound and added color Doppler images to this edition. 
Dr. Amgad N. Makaryus organized the ﬁ gures and co-authored 
the new chapter on cardiac computed tomography (CT) and 
revised chapter on cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) 
with Dr. Lawrence M. Boxt. Dr. John Guy Santilli once again 
has expertly added the new imaging modalities to his chapter 
on the thoracic aorta. Dr. Miller is indebted to Dr. Anand 
V. Soni for revising the valvular heart disease chapter. We 
thank each for their well-written and illustrated contributions.
Dr. Boxt thanks his son Elliot Fried-Boxt for his perseverance 
and uncanny organizational skills for archiving and cataloging 
terabytes of digital CT and MRI imagery, allowing him to choose 
appropriate imagery for the text.
Imaging in diagnostic radiology books requires accurate, as 
well as exquisite, illustrations. We are most grateful to Susanne 
L. Loomis, MS, FBCA, at the Radiology Educational Media 
Services of the Massachusetts General Hospital. Her artistic 
background and computer skills have immeasurably contributed 
to the superior quality of the ﬁ nal images. She is also the artist for 
the drawings throughout the book.
Producing a book requires a tremendous amount of adminis-
trative help to the authors from the publisher. Elsevier has once 
again demonstrated a superior organization to bring ideas from 
pen to published book. A round of applause is due to our acquisi-
tions editor, Rebecca S. Gaertner, and to several development 
editors who encouraged us with praise as well as deadlines. 
Thanks to Colleen McGonigal who added the ﬁ nishing touches 
and to David Saltzberg who oversaw production. We are inﬁ -
nitely grateful to Rhoda Bontrager, our hard-working production 
editor, who coordinated the contributions from all authors and 
oversaw a meticulous review of the text and ﬁ gures.
Stephen Wilmot Miller, MD
Lawrence M. Boxt, MD
Suhny Abbara, MD
