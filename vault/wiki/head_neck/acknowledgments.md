Acknowledgments
We would like to express our sincere gratitude to the dedi-
cated individuals who made the most important contributions 
to the development of this book. They include
The talented sonographers at Duke Radiology, the Duke 
Fetal Diagnostic Center, and the Mallinckrodt Institute of 
Radiology, whose skill and dedication to excellence led to the 
creation of many of the outstanding images in this edition of 
Ultrasound: The Requisites.
Dr. Leslie Scoutt of Yale University and Drs. Brita Boyd, 
Sarah Ellestad, and Elizabeth Livingston in Maternal and Fetal 
Medicine at the Duke Fetal Diagnostic Center for the time and 
effort they spent answering questions, sharing their vast 
knowledge, reviewing text, and identifying and/or contribut-
ing obstetric and vascular cases.
Nancy Stanforth, RDMS, for taking the time to share her 
collection of fetal cardiac case material and discuss various 
facets of fetal heart imaging.
Regan Matthews, Genetic Counselor, for providing cases, 
reviewing text, and sharing insights and expertise on chromo-
some abnormalities.
Jeffrey Hertzberg, for the time and effort he spent sharing 
his extensive knowledge and expertise in computers and 
graphic design during development of the book.
The many individuals at Elsevier who played a role in this 
project for their assistance in the development of Ultrasound: 
The Requisites, third edition. Special thanks to Margaret 
Nelson and Gabriela Benner, Content Development Specialists, 
who expertly guided us through the various stages in develop-
ment of the book and to David Stein, our project manager 
during the production stage, for their wisdom and guidance.
Dr. Al Kurtz for his exceptional leadership on the ﬁrst 
edition and his continued critical contributions on the second 
edition. Al’s wisdom and insight helped lay the foundation for 
the current edition of Ultrasound: The Requisites. We both 
are honored when we see our names next to his on the previ-
ous covers.
Barbara S. Hertzberg, MD, FACR
William D. Middleton, MD, FACR
