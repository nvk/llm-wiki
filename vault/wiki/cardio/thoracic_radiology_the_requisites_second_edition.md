Foreword
THE REQUISITES series is designed to provide core 
material in major subspecialty areas of radiology for use by 
residents and fellows during their training and by practicing 
radiologists seeking to review or expand their knowledge. 
These books have also been found of value by non imag­
ing specialists in their own respective areas of interest.

­particularly popular book in the requisites series. Thoracic 
imaging is part of the heart and soul of the specialty of radi­
ology and encompasses a number of the most commonly 
performed procedures in our specialty. The subject is of 
interest to every radiologist.
