## Key Radiological & Clinical Findings
- **Radiography (Plain Film):** Essential baseline imaging for Brain Parenchymal Infection; evaluates anatomical contours, alignment, lucencies, and gross calcifications.
- **Computed Tomography (CT):** Gold-standard modality for multiplanar delineation, density characterization, and staging of Brain Parenchymal Infection.
- **Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI):** B, Corresponding hyperintensity on diffusion-weighted image. linium T1-weighted image shows faint rim enhancement of right basal ganglia lesion.
- **Ultrasound / Doppler:** Real-time, non-ionizing dynamic assessment with color and spectral Doppler evaluation of vascular flow.

## Differential Diagnosis & High-Yield Pitfalls
- Diagnostic mimics sharing radiographic or cross-sectional morphology with Brain Parenchymal Infection.
- Key discriminator: Contrast enhancement patterns, tissue composition (fat, calcium, blood products), and anatomical distribution.
- Clinical correlation: Integration with patient presentation, laboratory biomarkers, and acuity dictates definitive diagnostic pathway.

parts of the world. It is the leading cause of seizures world­
wide and has a unique life cycle and imaging characteris­
